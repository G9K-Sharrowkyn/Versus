10. Character A Powers / Tools / Weaknesses
- Powers: Kryptonian strength, speed, flight, heat vision, freeze breath, and enhanced sensory perception.
- Tools: Tactical movement, range control, and rapid target acquisition.
- Tools: Solar Flare as a high-cost finishing option for New 52 Superman.
- Weaknesses: Kryptonite exposure, magic susceptibility, and red-sun power loss.
- Weaknesses: Solar Flare leaves a temporary vulnerability window if it does not end the fight.

12. Character B Powers / Tools / Weaknesses
- Powers: Extreme strength, durability, flight, Atomic Vision, and near-instant regeneration.
- Tools: Atomic Vision, 12,000-degree beams capable of burning through adamantium and killing Vision in a single shot.

- Weaknesses: Vulnerability to Argonite, the Kryptonite equivalent in his world.
- Weaknesses: Extreme arrogance. He often underestimates opponents and lets them land the first blow, which can be a mistake against someone in Superman's class.
