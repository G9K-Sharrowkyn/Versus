10. Character A Powers / Tools / Weaknesses
- Powers: Absolute immortality, strength capable of tearing Sentry apart, and control over the living Abyss through symbiotes.
- Tools: All-Black the Necrosword, a god-slaying blade, and an army of symbiote dragons.
- Weaknesses: "Light" (the Enigma Force / Uni-Power) is his antithesis, though ordinary suns he simply extinguishes.
- Weaknesses: Arrogance. Knull often underestimates opponents and allows them the first move because he believes they cannot hurt him anyway.

11. Character A Raw Feats
- Beheads a Celestial in one stroke and turns its head into a forge.
- Tears Sentry, Earth's mightiest hero, in half with his bare hands.
- Survived the Big Bang without a scratch.
- Slaughtered pantheons of gods across the universe.
- Covers the entire Earth in a symbiote swarm within minutes.

12. Character B Powers / Tools / Weaknesses
- Powers: The Odinforce, manipulation of matter, time, energy, and souls, blessings, and Asgardian magic.
- Tools: Destroyer Armor, with Celestial-level durability, and Odin's sword, the Odinsword.
- Weaknesses: Odin Sleep. Overusing the Odinforce forces him into a regenerative slumber.
- Weaknesses: Pride. Odin can be stubborn and too convinced of his own infallibility, which Knull may exploit.

13. Character B Raw Feats
- The narrator describes Odin as "Living Omnipotence."
- Holds back Surtur's flame, which could burn Yggdrasil (9 Realms = 9 universes).
- Is cited as a being capable of ending the Multiverse or creating a new one.
- His battle with Seth shakes every plane of reality, destroying galaxies as collateral damage.
- Stops time, tears the fabric of eternity, and casts a spell to the far end of the universe.
