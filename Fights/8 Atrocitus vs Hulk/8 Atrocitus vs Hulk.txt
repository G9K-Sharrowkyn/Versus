10. Character A Powers / Tools / Weaknesses
- Powers: Red Lantern plasma, blood magic, interdimensional detection, telepathic resistance, army creation (Manhunters).
- Tools: Red Power Ring, Divine Rod (power draining), Power Battery with the Butcher.
- Weaknesses: Dependency on the ring heart (it replaces his actual heart).
- Weaknesses: Extreme use of blood magic exhausts his life force.

11. Character A Raw Feats
- Burning a hole through reality itself to access other dimensions.
- Divine Rod – constructing a device capable of siphoning power from other Lantern rings.
- Creating a Red Lantern army from Manhunters using blood necromancy.
- Complete immunity to MMH's telepathy (MMH takes psychic damage upon attempt).
- Predicting the future via blood ritual, tracking "paths of fury."

12. Character B Powers / Tools / Weaknesses
- Powers: Gamma Emission (World Breaker), immortality (Green Door), atomic regeneration, Thunderclap affecting dimensional beings.
- Tools: None – he is the ultimate weapon himself.
- Weaknesses: Extreme gamma draining can revert him to Banner form.
- Weaknesses: Attacks targeting the soul directly may slow him down, though rarely stop him.

13. Character B Raw Feats

- Nearly sank the US East Coast with a single step.
- Withstood mystical energy equivalent to 133 maximum-force blows from Hercules.
- Energy Absorption – absorbing 5 gamma bombs simultaneously without overload.
- MFTL+ reaction speed allowing him to contend with Sentry and Thor.
