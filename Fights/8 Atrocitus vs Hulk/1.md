Summary
Atrocitus is a Red Lantern and an enemy of the Guardians of the Universe, and the Sinestro Corps. He was originally the leader of The Five Inversions, having joined the group after escaping the genocide committed by the Manhunters and watching them kill his family, and thereupon established the Empire of Tears that spanned a great swathe of space, until their eventual imprisonment and defeat. Atrocitus, however, convinced Abin Sur to free him by using his knowledge of the prophecy of the Blackest Night--later manipulating Abin Sur into experiencing fear, and ultimately mortally wounding Abin Sur, directly causing the events that would lead to Hal Jordan becoming the new Green Lantern of his Sector. Later, on Ysmault, he performed a blood ritual, slaying three of the four Inversions and using their blood to create the first Red Power Battery and ring. He then christened the battery in the last Inversion's blood, thus establishing the Red Lantern Corps.

Summary
Atros, also know as Atrocitus, is the fearsome leader of the Red Lantern Corps in the New 52 continuity of DC Comics. Originating from the planet Ryut in Space Sector 666, Atrocitus is driven by a profound sense of rage and vengeance following the massacre of his sector by the Manhunters, the rogue predecessors of the Green Lantern Corps. Channeling his anger into a mission of retribution, he creates the Red Lantern Corps, harnessing the Red Light of Rage to combat injustice and punish those he deems responsible for his suffering as the leading Red Lantern. Atrocitus's journey is marked by his relentless pursuit of vengeance, his leadership of the Red Lanterns, and his complex relationship with rage.

Atrocitus's abilities are derived from his Red Lantern ring, which channels the Red Light of Rage. This ring grants him superhuman strength, durability, and the ability to create powerful constructs made of red energy. The ring also allows him to fly, survive in space, and generate destructive energy blasts. Unique to the Red Lanterns, Atrocitus can vomit corrosive red plasma, capable of burning through almost anything. Additionally, the ring's rage-fueled energy enhances his physical abilities, making him nearly unstoppable in combat. His connection to the Red Light also grants him a form of immortality, as long as his rage continues to fuel his powers.

Atrocitus's relationships are central to his character and his role within the Red Lantern Corps. His primary motivation is his deep-seated hatred for the Guardians of the Universe and the Green Lantern Corps, whom he holds responsible for the destruction of his homeworld. This vendetta drives many of his actions and decisions. Within the Red Lantern Corps, Atrocitus's leadership is both respected and feared, as he commands loyalty through his immense power and unwavering resolve. His interactions with fellow Red Lanterns, such as Bleez and Dex-Starr, highlight the complex dynamics of a Corps fueled by rage. Despite his harsh demeanor, there are moments that reveal Atrocitus's capacity for empathy, particularly towards those who share his pain and anger.

In the New 52 continuity, Atrocitus's journey is marked by significant storylines that explore his role as the leader of the Red Lantern Corps and his ongoing quest for vengeance. In the "Red Lanterns" series, written by Peter Milligan and Charles Soule, Atrocitus faces various challenges to his leadership and the stability of the Corps. Key arcs include his battles against the Third Army and the First Lantern during the "Rise of the Third Army" and "Wrath of the First Lantern" storylines, where his rage-fueled powers are crucial in the fight against these formidable threats. In the "Lights Out" event, Atrocitus and the Red Lantern Corps join forces with other Lanterns to stop Relic, a powerful adversary threatening the entire Emotional Spectrum. Throughout these narratives, Atrocitus's character evolves as he grapples with the complexities of leadership, the consequences of his rage, and the pursuit of justice. His story in the New 52 is one of vengeance, power, and the unrelenting force of rage, making Atrocitus a compelling and formidable figure in the DC Universe.
