## Suno Notes

- Use `Custom` with `v4.5` or newer.
- Put genre, arrangement, vocal type, tempo, and sonic texture in `Style of Music`.
- Put the lyric sheet in `Lyrics`, but if the result feels too generic, add one short bracketed direction above the first verse.
- Avoid artist or band names in the prompt. Suno says names of well known artists or people can be flagged, so descriptive language is safer and usually works better.
- Use `Exclude` on purpose. It is the cleanest way to block bright pop sheen, trap hats, EDM risers, or other elements that break the mood.
- Good starting sliders for these songs: `Style Influence 70-80%`, `Weirdness 30-45%`. Raise `Weirdness` only if the result is too safe or too generic.

## 1. Quietly Disappear

I kept the porch light on through every winter
Like a lonely house still listening for your step
The floorboards keep the weight of your leaving
The bed stays cold no matter where I turn

[Pre-Chorus]

Like weather turning underneath my skin
Now I drink my coffee with the ghost of morning

[Chorus]
Come quietly disappear inside my arms
Like dusk dissolving softly into rain
If grief is just the last translation of love
Then let this quiet house keep saying your name
Come quietly disappear inside my arms
Like smoke returning to the shape of flame
I have no prayer that does not sound like you
No peace that does not sound the same

[Verse 2]
The flowers on the table bow by evening
I do not move them, let them fade in place
The house and I have learned the same surrender
To keep a shape for what we could not save

[Pre-Chorus]
By one blind touch I would have known you anywhere
In any dark, in any life, in death
Now all I have are photographs and doorways
Still opening on no one but the dark

[Bridge]
I do not cry, I bleed onto the paper
I do not heal, I only learn the ache
Home was never timber, lock, or window
Home was the hush that gathered when you stayed

[Final Chorus]
Come quietly disappear inside my arms
As smoke returns to where the fire has been
If love survives as ruin and remembrance
Then be the ghost this house still carries in

The most intimate song of the set: house-at-night grief, porch light, fading flowers, ghost warmth in the wood. This should feel devotional and wounded, with enough weight to touch doom metal territory without losing closeness.

[Suno Style Prompt]
Slow-burn melodic doom and atmospheric alternative metal elegy, around 72 BPM, intimate piano and clean guitar opening, brushed or very restrained drums, distant cello, warm bass, low-tuned guitars entering gradually in the chorus, close low male baritone with fragile breath and controlled sorrow, verses should feel like walking through an empty house that still remembers love, chorus should widen and ache without turning triumphant, cinematic but minimal, haunted, devotional, smoke, rain, wood, and old-room resonance, finish in resignation and near-silence rather than a big ending.

[Suno Exclude]
No trap hats, no EDM risers, no glossy synth-pop sheen, no cheerful uplift, no arena gang shouts, no pop claps, no bright radio-rock optimism.

[Suno Sliders]
Style Influence 78%. Weirdness 34%.
