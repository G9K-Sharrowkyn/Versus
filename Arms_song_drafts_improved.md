## 1. Quietly Disappear (Refined)

[Verse 1]
I kept the porch light on each winter evening
Like a house that listens for your step
The floor remembers every weight of leaving
The bed stays cold wherever I turn

[Pre-Chorus]
I felt you go in tiny changes
Like weather shifting under my skin
Now I drink my coffee with the ghost of morning
And teach my habit how to be a life

[Chorus]
Come quietly and disappear inside my arms
Like dusk dissolving slowly into rain
If grief's the final translation of our language
Then let this quiet house keep calling your name
Come quietly and disappear inside my arms
Like smoke that folds back into the flame
I have no prayer that doesn't sound like you
No peace that calls another name

[Verse 2]
The flowers on the table bow by evening
I do not move them; let them fall in place
The house and I have learned the same surrender
We keep the shape of what we could not save

[Bridge]
I do not cry; I spill onto the paper
I do not heal; I only map the ache
Home was never timber, lock, or window
Home was the hush that gathered when you stayed

[Final Chorus]
Come quietly and disappear inside my arms
As smoke returns where once the fire had been
If love survives as ruin and remembrance
Be the ghost this house keeps carrying within

## 2. Museum of Things I Want to Forget (Refined)

[Verse 1]
I keep your letters in the kitchen drawer
With dead batteries, receipts, a tarnished key
Small relics of a lonely kind of worship
No one would bless them, yet they're plain to see

[Verse 2]
I turned your absence into an exhibition
Lit every lie like candles in a row
The glass is clean, the labels read and careful
But every room still smells of wax and smoke

[Pre-Chorus]
Tell me, was it love
Or only fear of losing more?
Was I holding on to memory
Or only worshipping the cost?

[Chorus]
I am a museum of the things I want to forget
Hall after hall of smoke and silver frames
Each memory hangs like iron on my throat
Each quiet room keeps answering your name
I am a museum of the things I want to forget
Still open when the midnight visitors come
I know being haunted is not the same as love
But some nights it comes close enough

[Verse 3]
Of all I lost, I miss my old reflection
The face that didn't learn to plead or wait
I was not weak, I only kept mistaking
Endurance for the shape that love should take

[Bridge]
Tonight I walk the rooms with both hands empty
Take down the glass, the captions, and the ropes
I cannot keep curating my damage
And call the habit hope

[Final Chorus]
I am a museum of the things I want to forget
But I will not be anyone's monument to pain
Tonight I kill the lights, unchain the doors,
And try to learn my name again

## 3. Umbrella at World's End (Refined)

[Verse 1]
There was a time I held the sky above you
Stood in the flood to keep your breathing warm
If every road had broken at the horizon
I'd build you shelter out of storm

[Verse 2]
You were a star no human heart could carry
Too bright for earth, too merciless for sleep
I never learned what to do with heaven
When heaven will not stay and will not speak

[Pre-Chorus]
Still, somewhere in the thunder
Something in me knew
Love is not the hand that keeps you
Sometimes love lets you through

[Chorus]
At the end of the world
I'd hold an umbrella over your heart
Let the black rain fall, let the ocean rise
Let daylight come apart
If I cannot be your shelter
I will not become your storm
At the end of the world
I'll try to keep your memory warm

[Verse 3]
Now the room is full of weather I can't outlast
Your name still gathers on the windowpane
I used to think surviving meant forgetting
Now I know it only means I bear the rain

[Final Chorus]
At the end of the world
I'd hold an umbrella over your heart
The world did not end in fire;
It ended quietly in dark
If I cannot be your shelter
I will not make your leaving sin
At the end of the world
I fold my hands and let the weather in

## 4. Garden of the Unsaid (Refined)

[Verse 1]
In the silence between us whole seasons ruined
Words unsaid sweetened slowly into rot
I knew the shape of sorrow on your shoulders
You knew exactly where to bury what I was

[Verse 2]
Everything returns at night: the hall, the picture
The way you turned before you closed the door
I reach for you in dreams like ruined churches
For a god they no longer keep indoors

[Pre-Chorus]
How strange to be abandoned
By someone still alive
How strange to keep on tending
What did not survive

[Chorus]
In the garden of the unsaid
Black flowers open in my sleep
Every word we never gave each other
Has taken root in me
In the garden of the unsaid
Your name still blooms at night
Beautiful as sorrow
Merciless as light

[Bridge]
One day this might turn softer
Less flood, more rain; less blade, more scar
But tonight the roots are in me
And I leave them where they are

[Final Chorus]
In the garden of the unsaid
I no longer pull the roots
Some grief was made for keeping
Some love outlives the truth
In the garden of the unsaid
There is no cure, only room
I make my peace with what still flowers
Though it never should have bloomed
